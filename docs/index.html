Bass
controls

Four pads. Two dials. Nothing else.

1 · Pads

Tap selects that pad's single settings and starts the sequencer.
Double-tap, hold sends the dials to that pad's double settings. Release to return.

2 · Dials

Dials act only while a pad is held. With no pad held they do nothing.
Moving or holding a dial still while a pad is held records it.

3 · Settings

(mod wheel)		 LFO width / filter shift
(CC 74)
4 trance gate / octave loop window (1 or 2 bars) / aberration

4 · Record

Two pads hold: record fill. Each step the loop passes while held is marked.
Three pads hold: record unfill. Each step passed while held is removed.
Release stops recording. The loop plays what is set, with the recorded changes.

5 · Reset

Four pads at once: clear every recording and fill, and set every setting to its default. Stops the sequencer.

6 · Loop

The loop is at most two bars (eight beats). The loop window setting chooses one or two bars.
